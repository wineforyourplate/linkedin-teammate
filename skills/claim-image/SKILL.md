# Claim image

The single-image post: a claim on a picture that proves it. People stop for the claim and stay for the picture.

Before writing, follow "Before writing any piece" in the `content-kit` skill (voice and visuals both apply), including `/house/claim-image/` if it exists.

## The piece

- **The claim** (goes on the image): 10 words or fewer, an outcome or a true number with its denominator. Never an adjective doing a number's job.
- **The image**: a real screen that shows the claim is true (ideally with the number visible on it), or a 3D-world still. Name the file and say what on it proves the claim.
- **The caption**: one line, in the house voice.
- **The link**: in the reply or first comment, never on the image.

## Output

```
# Claim image: <title>

Claim: <10 words or fewer>
Image: <pod path> (what on it proves the claim)
Caption: <one line>
Link: <where it goes>
```
